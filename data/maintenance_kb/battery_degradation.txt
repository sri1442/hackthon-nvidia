FAULT: Battery State-of-Health (SoH) Degradation
GROUP: battery
PARAMETERS: battery_soh, battery_voltage_v, battery_temp_c, battery_current_a

ROOT CAUSES:
- Cell capacity fade from repeated deep-discharge cycles below 20% SoC.
- Elevated pack temperature accelerating electrolyte degradation.
- Cell imbalance across series groups causing early voltage sag under load.

DIAGNOSTIC INDICATORS:
- battery_soh trending below 0.75 with a consistent downward slope indicates
  progressive capacity fade rather than a transient sensor fault.
- battery_voltage_v sagging disproportionately to current draw suggests
  internal resistance increase (aging cells).
- battery_temp_c above 60C during normal charge/discharge cycles indicates
  thermal stress accelerating degradation; above 85C is a hard failure risk.

RECOMMENDED ACTIONS:
- SoH between 0.55-0.75 (warning): schedule battery pack inspection within
  next maintenance window (24-48h). Estimated repair time: 2-4 hours
  (pack swap) or 6-8 hours (cell-level diagnostics + partial replacement).
- SoH below 0.55 (critical): immediate action. Remove AGV from service.
  Pack replacement estimated at 3-4 hours including recalibration.
- If voltage sag correlates with high current draw, check for compromised
  cell interconnects before assuming full pack replacement is required.

SAFETY NOTES:
- Do not attempt hot-swap of battery packs while AGV is on an active route.
- Thermal runaway risk increases sharply above 85C — treat as emergency stop.
