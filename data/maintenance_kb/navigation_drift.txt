FAULT: Navigation Heading Drift / Encoder Slip
GROUP: navigation
PARAMETERS: nav_heading_error_deg, nav_localization_conf, nav_path_deviation_m

ROOT CAUSES:
- Wheel encoder slip accumulating dead-reckoning error over time.
- Wheel/tire wear changing effective rolling diameter, invalidating the
  encoder-to-distance calibration.
- Sensor fusion drift when localization confidence degrades (e.g. reduced
  lidar/landmark visibility) and the system relies more heavily on
  dead-reckoning.

DIAGNOSTIC INDICATORS:
- nav_heading_error_deg increasing linearly over time (not a step change)
  is the signature of accumulating encoder drift rather than a discrete
  sensor failure.
- nav_path_deviation_m rising in parallel with heading error confirms the
  drift is affecting actual path-following, not just an internal estimate.
- If nav_localization_conf drops sharply first and heading error follows,
  suspect a localization/sensor issue (lidar occlusion, landmark loss)
  rather than pure mechanical encoder drift.

RECOMMENDED ACTIONS:
- Heading error 5-12 deg (warning): schedule encoder recalibration and
  wheel/tire inspection within next maintenance window. Estimated 1-2 hours.
- Heading error above 12 deg (critical): immediate action — risk of
  collision or route deviation into restricted zones. Estimated 1-2 hours
  for recalibration, longer if wheel replacement is needed.
- Re-run the calibration routine after any wheel or tire replacement.

SAFETY NOTES:
- Do not allow continued autonomous operation once path deviation exceeds
  the hard threshold — risk of collision with fixed infrastructure or other
  AGVs on shared routes.
