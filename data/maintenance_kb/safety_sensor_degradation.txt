FAULT: Safety Sensor Response Degradation (E-Stop / Sensing)
GROUP: safety
PARAMETERS: safety_estop_resp_ms, safety_lidar_return_rate, safety_camera_fps

ROOT CAUSES:
- Emergency-stop circuit relay or actuator wear increasing response
  latency.
- Controller firmware/processing overload delaying the e-stop signal path.
- Lidar/camera degradation (dust accumulation, lens fouling, sensor aging)
  reducing return rate or frame rate, indirectly increasing effective
  response time to hazards.

DIAGNOSTIC INDICATORS:
- safety_estop_resp_ms rising linearly over time (drift, not a spike)
  indicates progressive relay/actuator wear rather than a one-off glitch.
- safety_lidar_return_rate dropping in parallel with e-stop latency
  suggests a shared cause (e.g. controller processing load) rather than
  two independent faults.
- safety_camera_fps dropping alone (without e-stop or lidar changes)
  usually points to a camera-specific hardware/driver issue.

RECOMMENDED ACTIONS:
- E-stop response 80-200ms (warning): schedule safety system inspection
  and relay/actuator test within next maintenance window. Estimated
  1-2 hours.
- E-stop response above 200ms (critical): immediate action — this is a
  safety-critical fault. Remove AGV from service until resolved. Estimated
  1-2 hours for relay replacement, longer if wiring harness is implicated.
- Clean lidar/camera optics as a first troubleshooting step before assuming
  a full sensor replacement is needed.

SAFETY NOTES:
- E-stop response time is the single most safety-critical parameter in
  the fleet. Any critical-severity alert on this parameter should trigger
  immediate removal from the active route, regardless of estimated repair
  time.
