FAULT: Bearing Spall / Outer Race Defect
GROUP: mechanical
PARAMETERS: mech_bearing_vib_g, mech_bearing_temp_c, mech_brake_resp_ms

ROOT CAUSES:
- Outer race spalling from cumulative fatigue loading (classic PHM 2012
  bearing run-to-failure pattern: sudden step-change in vibration amplitude).
- Inadequate lubrication leading to metal-to-metal contact and localized
  pitting.
- Contamination ingress (dust/debris) accelerating surface fatigue.

DIAGNOSTIC INDICATORS:
- mech_bearing_vib_g showing a sudden step-change (not gradual drift) is the
  signature of a spall event — the defect has already initiated and will
  propagate rapidly (hours, not days).
- A gradual vibration rise combined with mech_bearing_temp_c rising in
  parallel indicates progressive wear rather than a discrete spall event.
- mech_brake_resp_ms increasing alongside bearing vibration can indicate the
  defect is affecting drivetrain-adjacent components.

RECOMMENDED ACTIONS:
- Step-change detected (critical): immediate action. This pattern has a
  short remaining-useful-life window (often under 8 hours per PHM 2012
  bearing degradation curves). Replace bearing assembly — estimated 2-3
  hours.
- Gradual rise (warning): schedule replacement within the next maintenance
  window. Estimated 2-3 hours for bearing assembly replacement.
- Always inspect the adjacent race and housing for secondary damage before
  reassembly.

SAFETY NOTES:
- Do not continue operation once a step-change spall pattern is confirmed —
  risk of sudden mechanical seizure.
